# Charts

Open Charts inside an agent to view a market and work with saved chart layouts.

## Markets and layouts

- Choose an instrument and candle timeframe.
- Create, duplicate, rename, or delete layouts.
- Save notes for a layout.
- Show or hide trade markers, the current position, and open orders.

Account overlays are read-only. They do not place or change orders.

## Drawings

- Add horizontal and vertical lines, trend lines, rays, rectangles, arrows, text, and callouts.
- Edit annotations and lock them to prevent accidental changes.
- Hide drawings without deleting them.
- Use Magnet to snap anchors to candle prices.
- Use Keep drawing to place several objects with the same tool.

## Indicators and chat

- Add indicators and adjust their inputs. See [Indicators](Indicators.md).
- Open the chart chat panel to discuss the current chart with your agent.
- Ask the agent to read or edit chart annotations if its chart tools are enabled.

Layout notes and drawings belong to that layout. They are not automatically
published as research memories or agent-wide insights.
