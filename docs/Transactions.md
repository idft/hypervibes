# Transactions

Open Transactions to review the agent's linked Hyperliquid account history.

## Trades

- Review perpetual trades grouped from the opening of a position until it returns to zero.
- Check direction, status, entry and exit prices, and size.
- Compare realized profit or loss, fees, funding, and net realized results.
- Open a trade to inspect its individual fills.
- Add notes and read earlier notes.
- Follow recorded decision links when the app can match a fill to an agent order.

## Activity

- Review individual fills, funding payments, and account movements.
- See spot fills, which do not appear in the grouped perpetual Trades view.
- Check event times, fees, USDC changes, and calculated balances.
- Add notes to individual entries.

## History limits

Hyperliquid supplies only the latest 10,000 historical fills. The app retains
imported history, but older activity may be missing on a new installation.

Check synchronization warnings and incomplete coverage labels before relying
on totals. Activity balances reflect imported events, not a verified account
equity snapshot. An unmatched fill may come from manual trading or another app.
