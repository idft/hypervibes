# Dashboard

The Dashboard summarizes the selected agent's account and recent work.

- Check total equity and unrealized profit or loss.
- View balance history over 24 hours and 30 days.
- Review open positions and orders.
- Check sub-agent activity.
- Use the main conversation without leaving the overview.

Watch for connection errors and stale account data. Missing data does not mean
an account is empty.

## Close a position

Use **Close at market** beside a position to cancel that market's orders,
including protective stops, and attempt a reduce-only market close. This also
works while the agent is disabled.

Check the result: a close may partially fill or fail, even after protective
orders have been canceled. If the outcome is uncertain, check the account before
trying again.

Closing does not stop the agent, which may reopen the position. Use
[Emergency stop](Agents.md#emergency-stop-and-re-enable) first to halt its activity.

Use [Transactions](Transactions.md) for trade history and
[Charts](Charts.md) for market analysis.
